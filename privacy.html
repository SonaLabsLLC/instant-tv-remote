Privacy Policy

Instant TV Remote · Effective November 17, 2026

In short: Instant TV Remote does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app has no servers of its own.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your TVs, the app saves each TV's name, model, local IP address, network (MAC) address and the pairing token your TV issues. It also saves your app settings. This information is stored only on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Your local network

The app talks directly to TVs on your home Wi-Fi network. To find your TV it briefly checks addresses on your local network; this traffic never leaves your network. Commands such as volume, text you type with the keyboard feature, and app launches are sent only to the TV you chose.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app checks your purchases with Apple's StoreKit on your device. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
