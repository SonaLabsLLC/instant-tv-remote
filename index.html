Getting connected

  • Your iPhone and Samsung TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • When asked, allow Local Network access (Settings › Instant TV Remote › Local Network).
  • The first time you connect, choose Allow on the TV's pop-up. If you missed it, open the TV's Settings › General › External Device Manager › Device Connection Manager and allow "Instant TV Remote".
  • Can't find your TV? Add it by IP address: on the TV, open Settings › General › Network › Network Status › IP Settings.
  • To turn the TV on from the app, enable the TV's Power On with Mobile setting (Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings).

Purchases

  • The remote is free. Pro (Keyboard, App Shortcuts, Touchpad and Unlimited TVs) is a one-time Lifetime purchase or a Yearly subscription, each with a 3-day free trial.
  • Changed phones? Tap Settings › Restore Purchases in the app.
  • Manage or cancel the Yearly plan in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Supported: Samsung Smart TVs running Tizen (2016 and later).
